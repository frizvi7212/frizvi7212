### ⭐ Main Projects

#### 🧑‍🏫 Ustadji: AI tutor for C++ and IT

**Live:** [ustadji-edu.vercel.app](https://ustadji-edu.vercel.app)

Most AI tools give you the answer, and you learn nothing. Ustadji does the opposite. It asks structured questions that guide a student toward the solution themselves.

- **Guided questioning:** built around a Socratic teaching flow instead of direct answers
- **Weak-spot memory:** the tutor tracks mistakes a student keeps making. Every 10 messages, an LLM summarizes them into structured data on the user's profile, and spots the student hasn't struggled with for 3 weeks get dropped automatically
- **Assignment help:** helps students work through assignment questions without just solving them
- **Token-efficient conversations:** designed to keep LLM calls small and cheap
- **Auth and trial system:** custom authentication with a trial flow for new users

**Stack:** Django, Groq (LLM), Vercel (landing page)
**Status:** live and in early testing with a small group of users. Currently fixing UI issues from tester feedback.

#### 🧩 HonestR: focus extension for self-taught learners

**Link:** [HonestR](https://honesthour.vercel.app/)

A Chrome extension built for GatewayHacks 2026. Self-taught learners have no teacher or schedule keeping them on track, so distraction is the real barrier. HonestR is built around that problem.

- **Real activity detection:** tracks whether you're actually working, not just whether a tab is open
- **Study Mode:** a focus session with no early exit
- **Idle nudge:** a prompt when you drift away
- **To-do list** built into the extension
- **Local-only storage:** your data stays in your browser
